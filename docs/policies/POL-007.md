# POL-007: Injectable drug lines (HCPCS J codes)

id: POL-007

> SYNTHETIC POLICY. This is a synthetic policy written for demo and testing
> purposes only. It is NOT a CMS document, not a Medicare coverage
> determination, and not a payer document. The unit limits are invented for
> this project.

## Criteria

POL-007.1 Drug lines are billed in the HCPCS billing unit of the code: J3301 per 10 mg, J1100 per 1 mg, J1030 per 40 mg and J0696 per 250 mg.

POL-007.2 The clinical note must document the dose given. The units billed must equal the documented dose divided by the billing unit.

POL-007.3 The units billed on a drug line must not exceed the per-code daily limit that applies under POL-005.1.
