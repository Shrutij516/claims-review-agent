# POL-006: Modifier 25 on office visits billed with a procedure

id: POL-006

> SYNTHETIC POLICY. This is a synthetic policy written for demo and testing
> purposes only. It is NOT a CMS document, not a Medicare coverage
> determination, and not a payer document.

## Criteria

POL-006.1 When an office visit (CPT 99213, 99214 or 99215) is billed on the same date, for the same patient and the same provider, as a minor procedure (CPT 20610 or 96372), the office visit line requires modifier 25.

POL-006.2 Modifier 25 is appropriate only when the clinical note documents a significant, separately identifiable evaluation beyond the usual assessment for the procedure.

POL-006.3 An office visit billed without a same-day procedure does not need modifier 25.
