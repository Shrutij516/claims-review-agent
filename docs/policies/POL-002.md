# POL-002: Office visit level (CPT 99213 to 99215) versus documentation and diagnosis complexity

id: POL-002

> SYNTHETIC POLICY. This is a synthetic policy written for demo and testing
> purposes only. It is NOT a CMS document, not a Medicare coverage
> determination, and not a payer document. The diagnosis tiers it refers to
> are invented for this project.

## Criteria

POL-002.1 The visit level billed must be supported by the documented complexity of the visit. The diagnosis on the claim is the starting point, and the clinical note should not contradict it.

POL-002.2 For this synthetic ruleset, level 99213 requires at least one tier 1 diagnosis, level 99214 requires at least one tier 2 diagnosis, and level 99215 requires at least one tier 3 diagnosis. The tier of each diagnosis code is listed in data/v2/flag_catalog.json.

POL-002.3 A tier 3 diagnosis, for example heart failure or stage 4 chronic kidney disease, supports level 99215 even when the visit itself was short, provided the note documents active management of that condition.

POL-002.4 A routine general exam diagnosis (Z00.00) or an uncomplicated minor illness does not support level 99214 or 99215.
