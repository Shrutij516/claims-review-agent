# POL-003: Knee MRI (CPT 73721) and prior conservative therapy

id: POL-003

> SYNTHETIC POLICY. This is a synthetic policy written for demo and testing
> purposes only. It is NOT a CMS document, not a Medicare coverage
> determination, and not a payer document.

## Criteria

POL-003.1 A knee MRI requires at least 6 weeks of documented conservative therapy before the study. Conservative therapy means supervised or home physical therapy, anti-inflammatory medication, bracing, or activity modification.

POL-003.2 The clinical note must state the type and the duration of the conservative therapy tried. Notes that describe recent onset, therapy not yet started, or less than 6 weeks of therapy do not meet POL-003.1.

POL-003.3 Exception: conservative therapy is not required when the note documents a red flag. Red flags are a locked knee, suspected fracture, suspected infection, neurovascular compromise, or inability to bear weight after trauma.

POL-003.4 The study must identify which knee was imaged, either through an RT or LT modifier or through an ICD-10 code that itself specifies laterality.
