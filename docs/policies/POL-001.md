## Criteria

POL-001.1 Diagnosis specificity. The diagnosis must support medical necessity for a meniscectomy specifically: a documented meniscal tear or derangement (for example the ICD-10 M23.2 series). A diagnosis of degenerative osteoarthritis alone (for example the M17 series), with no documented meniscal tear, does not by itself support medical necessity for this procedure and is high risk for denial. A claim whose clinical note describes only degenerative findings is treated the same way.

POL-001.2 Laterality. The claim must indicate which knee (right or left) was treated. This can be shown either by a laterality modifier (LT or RT) on the procedure line or by an ICD-10 code that itself specifies laterality (many meniscus ICD-10 codes encode right or left in the code). A claim with no laterality evidence at all, meaning neither a modifier nor a laterality-specific diagnosis code, is subject to automatic denial.

POL-001.3 Compartment linkage. The diagnosis must be linked to the specific knee compartment treated (medial or lateral meniscus), not to a generic or unspecified knee diagnosis.
